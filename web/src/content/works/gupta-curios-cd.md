# CD Mechanical Storage System

![CD Mechanical Storage System](/works/blender/gupta-curios-cd.png)

A photorealistic, skeuomorphic 3D vintage mechanical CD storage tower featuring precision-machined brushed aluminum, spring-loaded push-button ejection mechanisms, and full 360-degree orbital interaction.

### Engineering & Visual Details
- **Mechanical Ejection Rigging**: Spring-loaded rack and pinion physics triggering a 40% jewel-case ejection slide upon push-button release.
- **Physical Material Shading**: Anisotropic brushed aluminum bevels, CNC mill chamfers, neon indicator status diodes, and optical polycarbonate disc reflections.
- **Interactive Orbit & Desk Environment**: Full orbital camera freedom exploring the solid machined backplate, ventilation grilles, and studio work desk context.

# Three Books. One Argument.

<video src="/works/books/all_books_showcase.mp4" autoplay muted loop playsinline controls style="width: 100%; border-radius: 12px; margin-bottom: 24px;"></video>

Vishwjeet Chavan's independent authorship examines albums and audiovisual works as complex artistic constructions shaped by cultural history, identity, technology, and social forces. Rather than isolated commercial entertainment, each book treats its subject through deep critical reading, musical analysis, and cultural context.

## Published Volumes

### 01. A Caterpillar's Requiem: To Pimp a Butterfly
An extended cultural inquiry into Kendrick Lamar's masterpiece. Developed over approximately three years, exploring questions of race, trauma, spiritual transformation, and music as political storytelling.

### 02. Inside Is Not an Exit: Bo Burnham's Inside
A critical and cultural reading examining digital confinement, artistic labour, the commodification of personal vulnerability, and the contradictions of creative life under platform capitalism.

### 03. Can We Get Much Higher: Kanye West's MBDTF
An analysis of celebrity, power, excess, and public identity through the sonic and visual architecture of *My Beautiful Dark Twisted Fantasy*.

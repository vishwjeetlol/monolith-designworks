# Indian PCO Yellow Payphone

![Indian PCO Yellow Payphone](/works/dev/pco-payphone.png)

An authentic, deeply skeuomorphic interactive simulation of the iconic Indian **PCO STD/ISD yellow coin-box telephone**.

### Technical Highlights
- **Procedural Web Audio Synthesis**: Fully synthesized DTMF dual-tone multi-frequency dialing, mechanical coin-drop acoustics, dial tones, line noise, and rotary relay switches with zero external audio samples.
- **Physical Coiled Cord & Handset**: Real-time Verlet physics simulating the heavy rubberized coiled cord, handset weight, spring-loaded cradle hooks, and physical hanging dynamics.
- **Authentic Mechanical Feedback**: Tactile click responses, coin rejection slots, LCD digital meter timer displays, and vintage STD/ISD rate calculation logic.
- **Interactive Hotline Audio**: Directory connections playing procedural announcements and retro audio streams.

# Personal Interactive Portfolio: The Indian Bazaar

An experimental personal portfolio inspired by the vibrant visual atmosphere, spatial density, and playful energy of an Indian bazaar.

- **GitHub Repository**: [vishwjeetlol/VishwjeetPortfolio](https://github.com/vishwjeetlol/VishwjeetPortfolio)
- **Live Deployment**: [vishejeetportfolio.vercel.app](https://vishejeetportfolio.vercel.app/)

## Interaction Philosophy
Rather than conforming to static corporate grids, visitors navigate a continuous panoramic environment filled with interactive stalls, hand-coded digital toys, and tactile objects. Sound is procedurally synthesized directly through the Web Audio API, creating an atmospheric sonic landscape that reacts directly to cursor interactions.

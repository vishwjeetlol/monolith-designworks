# A Caterpillar's Requiem: To Pimp a Butterfly

![Book Cover Concept](/works/books/cover-concept-A.png)

An independently authored book examining Kendrick Lamar's *To Pimp a Butterfly* through an extended cultural and artistic analysis.

The project explores the album's musical construction, narrative, lyrical themes, cultural references, and artistic identity. It approaches the album as a layered artistic work, examining its questions of freedom, identity, transformation, race, culture, and self-representation.

- **Research Period**: Developed over approximately three years
- **Focus Areas**: Musical & lyrical analysis, narrative architecture, social & political storytelling
- **Publisher**: Pothi Publishing

# Ajanta Photo Studio

![Ajanta Photo Studio Experience](/works/dev/ajanta-photo-studio.png)

A nostalgic browser journey recreating the atmosphere and craftsmanship of classic mid-century Indian portrait photo studios.

### Core Experience
- **Large-Format Bellows Camera**: Interactive brass focus wheels, wooden accordion bellows extension, shutter cocking lever, and ground glass optical preview.
- **Magnesium Flashbulb Ignition**: Procedural screen-flash luminescence with residual phosphor decay and authentic retro shutter acoustics.
- **Darkroom Print Developing**: Canvas-based real-time chemical bath simulation revealing silver-halide monochrome grain, sepia toning, and studio embossed stamp borders.
- **Cultural Identity**: Typographic homage to vernacular Indian studio signage, hand-painted backdrops, and portrait posture guides.

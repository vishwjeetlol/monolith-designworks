# Can We Get Much Higher: My Beautiful Dark Twisted Fantasy

![Cover Design](/works/books/book-cover.png)

An independent critical reading of Kanye West's *My Beautiful Dark Twisted Fantasy*, examining the album through close musical analysis and a broader inquiry into celebrity, power, ambition, and artistic self-representation.

The project investigates:
- Sonic maximalism, orchestral rap, and sample collage.
- The tensions between public disgrace, commercial exile, and artistic resurrection.
- Music as an architectural monument to ego and vulnerability.

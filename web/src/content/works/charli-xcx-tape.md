# Crash Test Archive: Charli XCX Cassette & Zine

![Cassette J-Card Spread 1](/works/blender/charli_cassette_1.png)

![Cassette J-Card Spread 2](/works/blender/charli_cassette_2.png)

An experimental physical music packaging and zine archive celebrating the electronic pop catalog of Charli XCX (spanning *True Romance*, *Pop 2*, *Vroom Vroom*, *Crash*, and *Brat*).

- **Typographic System**: High-contrast brutalist and rave typography with industrial barcode motifs and transparent plastic overlays.
- **Physical Format**: Designed as a foldable cassette J-card insert and booklet, combining track archives, lyrics, and production notes.

# HeatAtlas: Urban Heat Island Forecasting

<video src="/works/dev/heatatlas.mp4" autoplay muted loop playsinline controls style="width: 100%; border-radius: 12px; margin-bottom: 24px;"></video>

HeatAtlas is an interactive data visualization platform developed for a hackathon, designed to make urban heat island anomalies, surface temperatures, and environmental vulnerability intuitive and accessible.

- **GitHub Repository**: [vishwjeetlol/heatatlas](https://github.com/vishwjeetlol/heatatlas)
- **Live Deployment**: [heatatlas.vercel.app](https://heatatlas.vercel.app/)
- **Features**: Real-time layer toggles, thermal contour maps, time-slider forecasting, and microclimate risk indices.

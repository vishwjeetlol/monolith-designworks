# Inside Is Not an Exit: Bo Burnham's Inside & Outtakes

**Subtitle**: *Confinement, Critique, and the End of the Room*

![Full Wrap Concept](/works/books/concept_A_wrap.png)

An in-depth critical and cultural reading of Bo Burnham's *Inside* and *Inside Outtakes*, examining them as interconnected artistic works about digital confinement, artistic labour, and the contradictions of life under platform capitalism.

The book investigates:
- How personal creativity becomes platform content and vulnerability becomes commodified entertainment.
- Cinematography, lighting, editing, and recurring visual motifs inside the isolated room.
- The spectator's complicity in consuming digitally mediated suffering.
